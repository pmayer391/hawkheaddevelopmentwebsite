My Little Fire Fighter

My Little Fire Fighter is a free iOS application aimed at helping young children learn some of the most commonly used colors. The parents are allowed to select an image of their young one, so that they can then act like a fire fighter as they save a burning building from a swarm of firey colors.


A special thanks to all of these amazing websites for providing the sounds to this app.
  • Bensound
  • ZAPSPLAT
  • freeSFX
  • SoundBible

Piece For Piece

Piece For Piece is an iOS puzzle game designed to help build and challenge your intellect. Peserve through the different levels and see if you can make it to the end.
